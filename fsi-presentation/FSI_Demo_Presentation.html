Live Demo
Conversational AI — One Prompt to Personalized Action
Monica (Customer)
Hi, I'm calling about my mortgage. I saw rates dropped and I'm paying 6.2%. I've been a customer for 12 years and honestly I'm frustrated — I feel like new customers get better deals.
Bank Agent
I completely understand, Monica. Let me pull up your account. I can see your history with us and I want to make sure we find the right option for you today.
Monica (Customer)
I've already started looking at other banks. I need to know — can you do something about this rate or should I move my mortgage?
Step 1
Understand
Identity: Monica Chen, 12yr customer. Intent: Mortgage refinance. Urgency: High (churn risk)
Step 2
Ground
Credit: 780. LTV: 62%. ML score: High retention value. Segment: Premium Loyal
Step 3
Decide
ML model: Savings Account. ConvAI override: Mortgage Refinance at 4.8% + fee waiver
Step 4
Activate
Frontline terminal: real-time offer. Email follow-up. Branch manager notification
The AI improved the decision
The ML model recommended Savings Account. The conversation revealed Mortgage Refinance intent. The AI combined both signals to deliver the right offer at the right moment — preventing churn and deepening the relationship.
